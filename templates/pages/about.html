About Lala Sarees

Lala Sarees is a saree house on P Road, Kanpur. We sell sarees for every occasion: ready-to-wear drapes for parties, tissue and georgette for weddings, silk with woven zari for festivals and lighter sarees for the office.

We sell to families buying for a celebration and to boutiques and shop owners who stock our designs. Every order ships with a GST invoice, and every piece is checked by hand before it is packed.
